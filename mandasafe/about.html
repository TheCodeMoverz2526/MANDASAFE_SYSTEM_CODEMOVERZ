About MandaSafe

The system and the office behind it.

MandaSafe is the road incident mapping and analytics system of the Mandaluyong City Traffic Planning and Management Office (TPMO). Officers record every road incident in the RIMAS console; the system maps those records inside the city's 27 barangay boundaries, finds density hotspots, forecasts the coming month, and publishes all of it here for residents.

Our Mission

Reduce road incidents in Mandaluyong through accurate records, honest mapping and evidence-based traffic management.

How Predictions Work

A linear trend per barangay over the recorded monthly history, adjusted by a Random Forest severity model once enough varied outcomes exist. Hotspots use Gaussian kernel density estimation over incident coordinates.

Who Uses It

TPMO officers maintain the records in the admin console. Residents and barangay officials read the maps, hotspots and forecasts published here.
