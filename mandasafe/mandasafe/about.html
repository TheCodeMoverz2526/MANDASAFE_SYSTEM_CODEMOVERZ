About Us

About the MandaSafe system and the office behind it.

MandaSafe

MandaSafe is a road incident mapping and analytics system developed for the Mandaluyong City Traffic Planning and Management Office (TPMO). It centralizes incident reporting from residents and field personnel, maps every report inside the city limits, and turns that data into hotspots and a safety index that guide where the city intervenes next.

Our Mission

To reduce road incidents in Mandaluyong through timely reporting, accurate mapping, and evidence-based traffic management.

Our Vision

A safer, smarter and more livable Mandaluyong where every road user reaches their destination safely.

Our Community

Residents, barangay officials, enforcers and the LGU working from a single, shared view of the city's roads.
