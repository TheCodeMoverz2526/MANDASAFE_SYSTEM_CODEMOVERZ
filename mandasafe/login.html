Privacy Policy

MandaSafe — Mandaluyong Road Accident Mapping & Analytics System · Effective September 25, 2026

The Traffic Planning and Management Office (TPMO) of the City Government of Mandaluyong operates MandaSafe. We respect your privacy and process personal data in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and the issuances of the National Privacy Commission (NPC). This policy explains what we collect, why, how we protect it, and the rights you have over it.

1. Information we collect 2. How we use it 3. Legal basis 4. Sharing and disclosure 5. Browser storage 6. How we protect it 7. How long we keep it 8. Your rights 9. Changes to this policy 10. Contact us

1. Information we collect

Account information you give us. When you create an account: your full name, email address, mobile number and the password you choose. You can update your name, email and mobile number later from My Account.

Verification information. When you reset your password, we send a one-time code to the email address registered to your account. Administrator accounts also use an authenticator app; for these we keep a secret key that links the account to that app.

Account activity. When your account was created, when you last signed in, and the sign-in session your browser is using.

Technical information. The IP address a request comes from. We use it to limit repeated sign-in, sign-up and code requests, which protects accounts from being guessed.

Road accident records. The accident data shown in MandaSafe is recorded by TPMO personnel: date, time, barangay, road, location, accident type, severity and status. These records do not contain the names or contact details of anyone involved in an accident. Residents can view them; only authorized TPMO administrators can create, change or delete them.

2. How we use it

  • To create your account and confirm that the email address or mobile number belongs to you.
  • To sign you in, keep you signed in, and let you recover your account if you forget your password.
  • To give residents and TPMO personnel the correct level of access — residents can view, administrators can manage records and accounts.
  • To protect the system against misuse, such as repeated guessing of passwords or codes.
  • To produce the maps, hotspot analysis, safety index and forecasts, which are computed from accident records, not from personal account data.

We do not use your personal data for advertising or marketing, and we do not sell it.

3. Legal basis

We process your account data on the basis of your consent, which you give when you create an account, and as necessary to provide the service you asked for. Accident records are processed in the performance of the City's mandate over traffic management and public road safety.

4. Sharing and disclosure

We share personal data only with service providers that help us deliver the service, and only as much as they need:

  • Email delivery (Resend) — your email address and the verification code, to send the code to you.
  • SMS delivery (Vocotext or Twilio) — your mobile number and the verification message, to send the code to you.
  • Map, font and icon services — when you open MandaSafe your browser loads map tiles from OpenStreetMap and fonts and icons from Google Fonts and Cloudflare (cdnjs). Like any website request, these services receive your IP address. They do not receive your account details.

We may also disclose information when required by law, by a lawful order of a court or government authority, or when necessary to protect life, health or safety.

5. Browser storage

MandaSafe does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It keeps two small items in your browser's local storage: your sign-in session (so you stay signed in between pages) and whether you collapsed the side menu. Signing out removes the session from your browser and ends it on our server.

6. How we protect it

  • Passwords are stored only as secure one-way hashes; no one at TPMO can read your password.
  • Verification codes are stored as hashes, expire after 30 seconds, and allow a limited number of attempts.
  • Sign-up details are held encrypted until the code is confirmed; administrator authenticator keys are stored encrypted.
  • Administrator accounts need both a password and an authenticator-app code to sign in.
  • Sign-in sessions expire after 12 hours, and changing your password ends all your sessions.
  • Access to records and accounts is limited by role.

No system is completely secure. If a personal data breach occurs that is likely to put you at risk, we will notify you and the NPC as the law requires.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account data — for as long as your account exists. You may ask us to delete it.
  • Unfinished sign-ups and verification requests — deleted automatically, generally within about an hour after they expire.
  • Sign-in sessions — until you sign out or 12 hours pass, whichever comes first.
  • Accident records — kept as official TPMO records under the City's records-retention rules.

8. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to:

  • Be informed that your personal data is being processed, and how;
  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Correct inaccurate or outdated data — you can update most of it yourself in My Account;
  • Object to processing, or withdraw your consent;
  • Have your data erased or blocked, for example by closing your account;
  • Data portability — to get a copy of your data in a commonly used format;
  • Claim damages for harm caused by inaccurate, incomplete, unlawfully obtained or misused personal data; and
  • File a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

Withdrawing consent or closing your account means you will no longer be able to sign in to MandaSafe.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the system or the law changes. The effective date at the top will change, and significant changes will be announced in MandaSafe before they take effect.
