How MandaSafe Works

Officers Record

TPMO officers log every road accident in the RIMAS admin console — date, barangay, road, type and severity.

Mapped by Barangay

Each record is placed inside the city's official barangay boundaries, by coordinates or barangay centroid.

Hotspots Found

Gaussian kernel density estimation finds true density peaks instead of a simple street count.

Next Month Forecast

A linear trend per area, adjusted by a Random Forest severity model, projects the month ahead.

Residents Informed

Everything the office records is published here so residents can plan safer routes.
